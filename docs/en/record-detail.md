# Record Detail Charts

## What Record Details Contains

The Record details screen displays:

- Time range and duration
- Average power
- Battery change
- Screen-on and screen-off duration
- Power, battery, and temperature charts

## Interactive Bottom Legends

The chart has several legends at the bottom. The first three are interactive:

- Power
- Battery
- Temperature

The Screen on and Screen off legends only explain their colors and do not toggle chart content.

## Power Legend Behavior

Tapping the Power legend cycles through:

`Raw -> Fitted -> Hidden`

## Battery and Temperature Legend Behavior

- Tap Battery to hide or restore the battery curve
- Tap Temperature to hide or restore the temperature curve

To focus on one curve, hide the others by tapping their legends.

## When to Switch Legends

- To inspect instantaneous fluctuations, use `Raw`
- To view the overall trend, switch to `Fitted`
- To reduce visual noise, hide curves that are not currently relevant
