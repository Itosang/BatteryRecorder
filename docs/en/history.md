# History

## Entry

On the home screen, tap either:

- Charging summary
- Discharging summary

This opens the corresponding history list.

## Swipe Actions

History items support swiping in both directions:

- Swipe right to export
- Swipe left to delete

## Exporting a Record

Swipe a record to the right to reveal the export action.
You will be asked to choose an output location, and the record will be exported as a text file.

## Deleting a Record

Swipe a record to the left to reveal the delete action.
After deletion, the record is removed from the history list.

## Opening Record Details

Tap the content area of a history item to open Record details for that item and view statistics and charts.
